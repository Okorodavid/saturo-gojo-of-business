# Part 006: Power Phone Scripts, PDF432–439

All assigned text in this package was closely read in the single complete emitted chunk. The material consists of the final subject-index pages and the ebook's EULA pointer. It is not a new substantive selling chapter, and the index does not supply the missing context of the earlier techniques.

The index is useful because it separates several decision problems that a superficial summary could blur: opening a call, qualifying interest and competition, qualifying an influencer, identifying decision participants, requalifying later, handling first resistance, resolving a considered objection, and obtaining a useful follow-up commitment. Those distinctions should guide retrieval from the earlier chapter text when an actual business problem arises. Merely finding a matching objection label is insufficient to select a response.

PDF432–434 list separate script families for email, follow-up, influencers, openings, resistance and qualification. The same surface phrase can have different roles: a prospect only taking emails is an access issue, while a qualified buyer needing a manager's decision is a buying-process issue. This package names the topics but does not prove what causes either situation.

PDF435–437 connect preparation and review with recurrent selling situations. The ten-characteristic index covers commitment, recurring situations, call critique, qualification, requalification, rapport, repeated asks, gatekeeper respect, peer environment and attitude. It also indexes pressure-oriented concepts such as asking at least five times, yes momentum, and treating requests to think or consult as stalls. Those entries need the full passages and buyer context; their presence in an index is not evidence that repetition should override a refusal.

PDF437 gives the sequence of the sample touch-point plan: an email after a voicemail, another email after a second voicemail, further voicemail spacing and a final voicemail. This is a retrieval outline, not a universal outreach cadence or authorization to contact anyone. Its timing should not override the owner's current sending preferences or a recipient's response.

PDF438 ends with in-house, all-set, existing-supplier and prior-failure objections. PDF439 points to an online publisher EULA; no terms were inferred and no external instructions were followed. There is no substantive chapter body left in this package. Earlier book portions remain assigned separately, and completing this index packet does not complete Power Phone Scripts by itself.
