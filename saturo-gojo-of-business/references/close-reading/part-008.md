## Serving and incentives

PDF149 challenges the assumption that every salesperson requires individual commissions. The Microchip example describes changing a60/40 salary/commission arrangement to90%base and10%variable tied to company growth, with reported higher sales, stable sales cost, reduced attrition and continued profitability. The author explicitly declines to prescribe eliminating commissions everywhere. The mechanism offered is less gaming, less internal conflict, more collaboration and greater alignment with customer service. A single reported company history does not isolate causation, establish the right compensation mix for another business, or supply current company results. For advice, examine the behavior an incentive produces, not just whether commissions are conventional.

PDF149–150 uses favor framing: approach the transaction as if the other person is doing the favor. The purpose is humility and perspective-taking, not surrendering price or delivery boundaries. Its commercial usefulness depends on whether the interaction genuinely helps both sides.

PDF150–153 distinguishes informational/rule signs from personal or purposeful signs. Museum queue reassurance addresses an immediate worry. A church's dog-waste sign links the rule to children playing on the lawn. These show two different routes: recognize the recipient's experience, or make the consequence for someone else visible. OCR misreads some short words; the surrounding prose supplies the distinction. This is not proof that every business needs more helper copy, and it does not override the owner's product-design preferences.

PDF153–154 proposes personalizing an anonymous buyer as one's grandmother, with the reputational variation that she has a large public audience. The underlying standard is what treatment one would consider acceptable for someone one cares about. The final two questions ask whether acceptance improves the buyer's life and whether the interaction improves the world. They are ethical decision tests, not conversion formulas or empirically calibrated measurement scales.

## What the supporting material adds

The acknowledgments on PDF155 describe editorial and methodological assistance and repeated reading. They attribute assistance; they are not selling instructions. The endnotes on156–164 make the book's evidential routes more precise. Workforce claims depend on occupation versus sector categories, exclusions of self-employed workers and historical2011–2012 observations. Forecasts to2020 or2016 are historical forecasts, not current verified facts.

The attunement notes separately cite perspective-taking, empathy, mimicry, reputation costs of poorly judged mirroring, personality and ambiversion. Thus the apparent advice to mirror behavior has cited boundaries within the book's own apparatus. Buoyancy cites interrogative self-talk, self-determination, emotion, positivity and explanatory style. Clarity cites both problem-finding research and criticism of the distinction, followed by framing, choices, combinations, experience and potential. These attributions support future investigation; reading a bibliography does not independently validate each study or its later scientific standing.

The pitch notes identify different evidential routes for questions, rhyme, email subject lines, microblogs and narrative. Improvisation draws on organizational research, theatre practice, listening and adaptive selling. Serve points to road-safety intervention, patient photographs, patient-focused hygiene messages, prosocial motivation, task significance and servant leadership. Keep experimental findings, journalistic accounts, biographies, practitioner frameworks and author interviews distinct rather than assigning them identical evidential weight.
