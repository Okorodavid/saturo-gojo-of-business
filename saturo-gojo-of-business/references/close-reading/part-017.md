# Part017: The New Strategic Selling, PDF316–360

All four assigned chunks were read in full, including the remainder of chapter18, chapters19–21, all fifteen follow-up questions and the company/publisher promotional material and cover OCR. Earlier book passages are assigned separately or have prior reading credit. File-page references below must not be replaced with printed page numbers.

## Allocation and funnel reassessment

PDF316–323 makes allocation conditional on more than the number of objectives at a stage. Deal complexity, immediate revenue, account potential, customer buying cycles and company product quotas can change the preferred distribution. The exceptional account pursued for years is explicitly not recommended as standard practice; elsewhere stale prospects are dropped. Do not extract only the success story to justify indefinite pursuit.

The workshop first estimates task distribution, then adjusts for those factors, compares ideal with actual work and revises actions. Best Few, Universe, Above the Funnel and In the Funnel each demand different work: finish legitimate end tasks, identify plausible accounts, verify suggestive need and cover actual buying bases. Comparing dated snapshots can reveal weak qualification, an empty future pipeline, delayed closing or a wrongly classified objective. A deal that remains Best Few through several checks may never have earned that stage.

The4–5%,10–15%,20–80%and90%probabilities are presented as rough framework guidelines. They do not supply calibrated close probabilities for our own market. Their value is to connect uncertainty with completed buying work, not to manufacture accurate forecasts from labels. Analysis cadence also depends on sales-cycle speed; the text's fortnightly starting practice and monthly minimum are contextual training recommendations.

## Turning strategy into action

PDF325–335 requires every planned action to leverage a Strength, reduce a Red Flag, or both. Actions should specify who is involved, where/when it occurs and what information will be confirmed, invalidated or obtained. A stalled proposed pilot may require learning the decision process and revising the target date rather than more confident reminders of an unrealistic deadline.

Covering a buying role is not the same as knowing a name. Coaching, a peer executive or an internal technical colleague may be better placed to resolve uncertainty than the salesperson personally. The seller also needs a business reason for the buyer to spend time: information useful to that buyer, not merely a request to advance the seller's pipeline.

Response Modes are explicitly changing perceptions of a situation, not personality types. A person's Growth or Trouble can matter differently from the company's overall growth. Corporate Results and individual Wins must be distinguished, while the text admits not everyone can win equally and some deals close without all motives known. Minimize losses and investigate concerns; do not pretend every sacrifice is a win or fabricate motives to fill a chart.

Competition includes doing nothing, allocating resources elsewhere and an in-house approach. Differentiation may be support, people, implementation knowledge or other genuine contribution beyond the product. The short next-action list is preferably four or five logical, urgent, doable items for the next one or two calls. It is updated when the call changes the facts. Comfort or gut relief is offered as a final acid test, but relief cannot independently establish that information is true or that the action is wise.

## How much planning is enough?

PDF336–342 rejects spending an hour on every ordinary interaction. Full analysis is for major value, important new markets, uncertainty, inherited accounts, difficult competition and stalled deals. The ten-minute version checks Buying Influences, their Win-Results, Red Flags/Strengths and a reliable Coach. The elevator emergency version at least identifies who influences the objective and who controls funding, or recognizes that these are unknown. The author explicitly says these shortcuts are not substitutes when a detailed assessment is required. The broader point is proportionate preparation and awareness of missing information, not a ritual duration.

Chapter21(PDF343–344) links a visible repeatable method with constant reassessment. Repeat use may improve speed, but reported awards and favorable participant surveys do not isolate a causal effect or eliminate uncertainty.

## The fifteen questions supply important boundaries

PDF345–354 adds qualifications easy to miss in a quick framework summary. Start where credibility actually exists, not automatically with the most senior title. A one-contact account is fragile when that person leaves; develop relationships without assuming everyone is presently influential. Verify authority through the process and required approvals rather than an individual's assertion or apparent confidence. Treat low-influence contacts respectfully because assessments and roles change.

Internal production, design and support staff are also Buying Influences when delivery depends on them. Same-team assumptions do not establish their capacity or commitment. For indirect channels, end-user influence depends on the particular sale: a consumer may not affect component procurement, while a large institutional customer's specifications may. The operative word is may, not a universal hierarchy.

A genuine Coach is defined as wanting this particular solution adopted; an information giver may serve several vendors. Cross-check the information's accuracy and usefulness rather than assign trust solely from friendliness or exclusivity. When a higher-priced product truly has no broader difference in value, the source acknowledges the commodity problem. Leadership does not remove the need to understand competing alternatives or customer concerns.

The presentation question recommends advance dialogue, or early interaction and feedback if a comparative demonstration cannot be avoided. Win-Results differ from generic product benefits because they concern one person at one time. The poor-fit customer answer admits revenue constraints: sometimes the choice is a conscious calculated risk with discrepancies anticipated, not an immediate refusal of every imperfect account.

The final answers reject guaranteed closing tricks and place credibility ahead of persistence. Their suggestion that needing to ask for the order indicates a flawed process conflicts with the explicit-ask emphasis in other supplied books. Preserve the shared logic of earned readiness and mutual understanding while still making purchase agreement explicit; neither silence nor a smooth conversation establishes consent. The book itself qualifies almost automatic closing as not magic.

## Source limits and added understanding

PDF355–360 promotes the training business, client list, benchmarking products, workshops, offices and historical leadership. These are source claims and commercial context, not present verified capabilities or instructions to contact them. Cover OCR supplies names and branding, with occasional recognition artifacts.

This fuller reading adds proportionate planning, internal commitment, channel contingencies and the reality of calculated poor-fit business to the initial six-element summary. It also exposes potential overconfidence in stage probabilities, gut comfort and near-automatic closing. Use the framework to make uncertainty visible and choose useful work, then test the analysis against what actually happens.
